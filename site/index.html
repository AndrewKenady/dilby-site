How it works

Running in an afternoon. Useful for years.

  1. Install it on one computer

    Run the Windows app, and Eli sets up its model and engine for your graphics card. After that first download it needs no internet at all.

  2. Open it to your network

    Flip one switch and every phone, tablet and laptop on your Wi-Fi can use Eli in a browser. You approve each person who joins.

  3. Eli remembers together

    Facts one person teaches Eli help everyone else, with the author credited. Health, money and anything personal stay with the person who said it.

Shared memory

Local AI tools treat everyone as alone. Eli treats your network as a household.

Ollama, LM Studio and Open WebUI give each person their own island. Eli keeps one memory for the group, and every fact records who taught it.

  • Facts, never transcriptsEli shares short things it learned ("Sam is allergic to peanuts"), not your conversations.
  • Sensitive topics stay privateHealth, money, relationships and anything about another person are kept for their author only.
  • Every fact has an authorEach person has their own token, so everything Eli learns is signed. Forget a fact and it disappears everywhere at once.
  • You can see what's sharedA Memory page lists what Eli knows from you, with one-tap make-private and forget. Replies credit others inline, like "(from Sam)".
  • Private chats in one tapA private conversation neither learns nor uses shared memory. Sharing can also be switched off per person, or for the whole hub.

Questions

FAQ

Does it need the internet?

Only once, to download the model (about 23 GB) during setup. After that Eli runs with the network cable unplugged. Offline install bundles for air-gapped sites are planned.

What hardware do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC with an NVIDIA graphics card with 12 GB of memory (AMD and Intel cards work through Vulkan, more slowly), 32 GB of RAM and about 25 GB of free disk. On a 12 GB laptop GPU it writes about 35 tokens (roughly 25 words) a second. Linux and macOS work from source.

Is it free?

Yes, for homes and communities of up to 10 people, with every feature a household uses. Organizations will pay for team controls, sign-in, scale and support; pricing isn't set yet.

What gets shared between people?

Short facts Eli learns, like where the spare key is or who is vegetarian, credited to whoever said them. Never whole chats, and never health, money, relationships or things about other people. You can see and forget everything Eli knows from you, and private chats share nothing.

How is it different from Ollama or Open WebUI?

Those are great tools for one person. Eli is built for a group: one install serves everyone on your network, the host approves who joins, and Eli keeps a shared memory with authorship instead of starting from zero for each person.
